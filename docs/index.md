# Self-Driving & Self-Balancing Bicycle

Welcome to the official research and documentation repository for our student project at **KU Leuven Campus Gent (Academic Year 2026–2027)**.

## Project Overview

Building upon the success of a [previous project](https://github.com/kayensmet/Self-driving-bicycle-2025-2026), where our predecessors engineered a self-balancing bicycle utilizing drone motors and a custom remote control, our team has been tasked with reevaluating, refining, and expanding the system's capabilities.

## Key Objectives & Enhancements

We are taking the project a step further by introducing advanced autonomous features:
- **GPS Trajectory Control:** Enabling autonomous navigation along predefined routes.
- **Obstacle Detection:** Implementing sensors and algorithms to identify and avoid hazards in real time.
